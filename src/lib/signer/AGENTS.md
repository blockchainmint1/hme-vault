# Signer rules

- Seeds delegation request authentication lives in `seeds-request.ts`, with immutable validated fields, fixed-order v2 canonical bytes and a release-pinned trust map; the production reader never accepts injected trust. Why: recipient identity must be verified offline before any later private-key derivation, without changing payment signer dependencies.

- QR collectors bound cumulative unique chunk characters before storing for both HMQR2 and legacy HMQR; duplicates do not consume the budget. Why: incomplete hostile streams must not bypass the assembled-text limit.
- Outgoing PSBT and EVM signed QR displays explicitly select the signing chunk policy rather than changing shared transfer defaults. Why: webcam tuning must not alter backup exports or incoming wallet requests.

- Offline signing (`src/lib/signer`) requires explicit PSBT network selection (TXC legacy coin-0 remains TXC only when selected), verifies every input fingerprint/path/script and SIGHASH_ALL, and permits EVM native/ERC-20 transfers only with chain-bound fees and matching sender; signed raw hex leaves by QR. Why: PSBT has no chain ID, and blind calls or unverified fees defeat offline review.
- EVM signing uses local viem account signing, strict versioned transfer intents and locally encoded ERC-20 calldata; every EVM address accepts lowercase or a valid EIP-55 checksum, and every `hm-evm-tx` on chains 1/56/8453/90031273 enforces the frozen 0.02 ETH / 0.02 BNB / 0.002 ETH / 0.02 ZCU fee ceilings pinned in `EVM_FEE_CEILINGS` before review and signing. Unknown networks/tokens display raw units and retain fee warnings. Why: the vault cannot fetch trusted metadata, corrupted mixed-case addresses must fail, and warnings alone must not permit excessive fees or opaque contract calls.
- PSBT review blocks unknown data, permits one zero-value TXC/ISK Omni Simple Send, TXC-only TSD Send To Many, or validated THORChain swap memo data output, checks the frozen per-coin cap table (`MAX_FEE_RATES`: 5,000 base units/vB for BTC/LTC/TXC/ISK, 1,000,000 for DOGE) before approval and after finalization, warns at 80% of the cap, labels the last non-change output as the Simple Send recipient, and binds signing to the reviewed bytes and keys using a private in-memory approval. Sign a clone with no fee bypass. Why: unreadable swaps are blind signing, Bitcoin defaults must not silently govern retries, and changed or failed requests must never reuse approval.
- Type-7 parsing lives in `omni.ts` and accepts only canonical version-0/property-39 packets with 1–7 positive-amount, unique vout mappings; review rejects missing/data/change/zero-value targets and displays every mapped address and amount. Why: multi-send cannot use Simple Send's last-output recipient inference; ISK activation and other token semantics are not established by TXC documentation.
- THORChain swap policy lives in `thor.ts` (pinned routers/tokens/decimals, memo grammar, payout-address and UTXO-shape checks) and `thor-evm.ts` (the `hm-thor-swap` intent, calldata rebuild and EVM fee ceilings); memos must be exactly four fields ending `/1/1` with no affiliate or aggregator tag, and EVM calldata is rebuilt locally and must match the request byte for byte. Why: the vault cannot fetch a quote, a pool address or a token list, so every swap value it relies on must be frozen in the app or refused.
- EVM bulk payments (`native-disperse`) allow only `disperseEther` on the pinned Disperse address, chains 1/56/8453, 1–50 unique recipients, sum-exact value and per-recipient gas bounds; calldata is rebuilt locally and signing waits for the full list to be scrolled and confirmed. Dated per-chain eth_getCode Keccak-256 hashes plus matching metadata-stripped executable-code hashes are pinned as release evidence, and the review warns that one unreceivable recipient reverts the whole batch. Review labels every recipient as this seed's own m/44'/60'/0'/0/0–999 address or, in red, not one of its addresses (public-only derivation in `own-addresses.ts`), with a count summary; labels never refuse a payment and no request field can switch them off. Bulk fee ceilings are the ordinary chain ceiling plus 0.0005 ETH/BNB per recipient on Ethereum/BNB or 0.00005 ETH per recipient on Base; single transfers keep the flat ceiling. Why: one contract call can hide many payees, a code-less value recipient can capture the whole batch, realistic batches need bounded per-recipient gas, and nothing supplied by the wallet is signed without complete review.
- Batch sweeps (`sweep-batch`, `sweep.ts`) accept only native/ERC-20 transfers on chains 1/56/8453/90031273, 1–25 items, one fingerprint and one destination (ERC-20 `to` = destination), paths m/44'/60'/0'/0/0–999 verified against derivation, consecutive per-address nonces with the native item last, a whole-batch fee ceiling of 5× the single ceiling (0.1 ZCU on Zero Chill), review that verifies senders and own-address ownership from the public-only branch so no private key is derived before signing, and all-or-nothing approval after the full list is scrolled (external destinations need a second confirmation). The reply is `hm-evm-signed-batch` with base64 transactions and keccak256 over the exact received bytes, shown in signing-size QR chunks. Why: one approval authorizes many transactions, so every item must be bound to the reviewed bytes and a single owner-confirmed destination.
- Branch access (`branch-access.ts`, shown to owners as "Share a trading pocket") is one profile-driven core, not a screen per service: a profile pins its request type, reviewed `productionTrust`, a `BranchPolicy` (allowed pockets plus exact ordered path list) and answer builder; `branch-policy.ts` refuses any node that is not fully hardened, shallower than depth 3, on account 0, outside the policy's pockets, or an ancestor of another requested node, a profile whose own policy fails these rules never loads, request scope must equal the policy before the review screen (no widening or partial approval), the derived path list must equal the approved list before signing, a seed's stored BIP39 passphrase is passed unchanged to Seeds Key's own `coinIdentityKey`/`buildHandoff` (which NFKD-normalise it once for identity and every family, Cardano included) and is never placed in the answer, with no Vault-written passphrase derivation, the shared `branchConsequence` sentences are mandatory on the review screen, `buildBranchAnswer` refuses unless the owner typed the seed's exact name (as for Give a copy), the modules copied from Seeds Key are SHA-256 pinned in `src/lib/pockets/copied-files.test.ts` and may change only by re-copying plus re-running the cross-project vectors, and a reviewed-key table test must change with any pinned key; and `requireLive` re-checks the request's expiry, path list and key fingerprint immediately before any secret is sealed; time-window checks allow exactly `SEEDS_CLOCK_TOLERANCE_MS` of phone clock error either way, and the screen's deadline is expiry plus that tolerance. Each approval is a `HandoverGuard` ticket bound to the exact request object and seed id; Cancel, closing or locking (unmount), changing seed, expiry and a new approval all cancel it, and `handOverPocket` checks the ticket after derivation, after the record save and again after a final liveness re-check, so no stale ticket ever displays an answer (a cancel during the record save may leave a record with no answer shown, which errs on the side of caution). Only pocket 1106 is offered, hands-off seeds are refused, the answer is sealed to the request's own recipient key, and the answer itself is never persisted; a non-secret `pocketShares` record is saved before the answer is shown, and if that save fails the answer is not shown. Binding the request hash as sealed-answer associated data is reserved for the next contract version. The answer body also carries a display-only `vault_fingerprint` (recomputed with `seedFingerprint`, never read from storage) and `vault_label_signature` built with the byte-pinned copy of Seeds Key's `vault-label.ts`, signed by the same identity over the full hand-over message, and the screens show the owner that ID. Why: each new ecosystem service must not fork its own derivation, trust or lifetime handling.
- Production branch-access trust is `SEEDS_REQUEST_KEYS` (exposed as `SEEDS_BRANCH_PROFILE.productionTrust`) and holds only keys verified out of band against SEEDS' release record, the live signer endpoint (checked by a person, never fetched by Vault), the owner's own fingerprint reading and a recomputed SHA-256; a test pins every id, value and fingerprint and verifies SEEDS' production-signed example at its fixed clock. Rotation adds a new id beside the old one in a reviewed release and removes the old one later; values are never edited in place. A request signed by any other key fails closed with the "doesn't trust" message, and test keys may only be injected through the profile's own test seam. Why: a QR that carries its own key cannot establish who issued it, and a live service must not be able to collect a pocket by forging a request.
